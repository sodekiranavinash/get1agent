## Support

How to reach the team and what to include in your message.

Support is the hub for help. It links to email support, the security team, the live status page and this documentation.

1. Open Support from the footer or the sidebar.
2. Choose a channel: email support for account, billing and product questions; security for vulnerabilities; the status page to check availability first.
3. In your message, include your User ID (from Settings), the affected resource name or id, and the approximate time of the issue.

- Support is best-effort — there is no guaranteed response time.
- Security reports are read by the security team and are one-way.
