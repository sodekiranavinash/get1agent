## Workflows

Your saved multi-agent workflows: open, inspect or delete them.

The Workflows page lists your saved workflows as cards showing the mode (graph or swarm), the number of agents and nodes, whether it is scheduled, whether it is verified, and when it last ran.

1. Click New workflow to start a fresh workflow in the builder.
2. Click Open on a card to edit it in the Workflow builder.
3. Use the trash button to delete a workflow. Deleting a workflow never deletes its agents.

- Workflows are private for now — there is no public workflow library.
- A “scheduled” chip appears on cards that carry a schedule; the Schedules page lists the details.
